Abstract

The 2024 E-Commerce Statistics Publication presents the development of E-Commerce in Indonesia from the perspective of E-Commerce businesses. The data presented in this publication includes business profiles, worker characteristics, business activities, and E-Commerce transaction value during 2024. Tabulation and analysis of survey results are presented in the form of estimates at the provincial level.
