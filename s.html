test
/tĕst/
intransitive verb
  1. To subject to a test; try.
    tested the pen by scribbling on scrap paper; testing job applicants.
  2. To determine the presence or properties of (a substance).
  3. To assay (metal) in a cupel.
  4. To undergo a test.
  5. To administer a test.
    test for acid content; test for the presence of an antibody.
  6. To achieve a score or rating on tests.
    tested high on the entrance exams.
  7. To exhibit a given characteristic when subjected to a test.
    test positive for the tubercle bacillus.
noun
  1. A hard external covering, as that of certain amoebas, dinoflagellates, and sea urchins.
  2. A procedure for critical evaluation; a means of determining the presence, quality, or truth of something; a trial.
    a test of one's eyesight; subjecting a hypothesis to a test; a test of an athlete's endurance.
  3. A series of questions, problems, or physical responses designed to determine knowledge, intelligence, or ability.
  4. A basis for evaluation or judgment.
  5. (Chemistry) A physical or chemical change by which a substance may be detected or its properties ascertained.
  6. A cupel.
